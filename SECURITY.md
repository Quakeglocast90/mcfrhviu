## 🔍 FAQ

<details>
<summary>What is FF Tracking?</summary>
FF Tracking is a Free Fire player statistics tracking tool providing match history, KDA analysis, rank tracking, weapon stats, survival rate, and clan analytics to help players improve gameplay and monitor competitive progress.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is FF Tracking safe to use?</summary>
Yes, FF Tracking is read-only statistics viewer using public Garena API. Does not modify game files or memory. Cannot cause bans—no game interaction, only stat viewing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I track other players?</summary>
Yes, enter any player's Free Fire UID to view their public statistics. Private profiles may have limited data. Used by clans for recruiting and by players to analyze opponents.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How often do stats update?</summary>
Stats update every 1-2 hours from Garena API. Manual refresh available in tracker. Real-time tracking not possible due to API rate limits—stats reflect recent matches with slight delay.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does this work with Free Fire MAX?</summary>
Yes, FF Tracking supports both Free Fire and Free Fire MAX. Stats shared between games—use same UID for tracking across both versions.
</details>
